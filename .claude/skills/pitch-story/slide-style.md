# Slide style

Your taste, not Claude's. `/pitch-story` reads this file and styles every slide to match it.

## Colours (1 background, 1 text, 1 accent)
- [x] Match the app: Paper #F4EFE4 / Ink #1D1B16 / Tomato #D9482B

## Background
- [x] Flat colour, no gradient

## Fonts
- [x] Same fonts as the app (Georgia headings, system sans body)

## Words per slide
- [x] Max 6 words in a title, max 3 short bullets per slide

## Avoid (AI slop)
- [x] Purple-blue gradients and glassmorphism
- [x] Emoji as icons
- [x] Stock photos of people pointing at laptops
- [x] "Revolutionising", "seamless", "leverage" and other filler
- [x] Clip-art chore icons: use a real screenshot of the wheel instead

## Sites or decks we like (links + what we like about them)
- https://linear.app — lots of empty space, one idea per screen
- https://stripe.com/sessions — big numbers, short sentences
