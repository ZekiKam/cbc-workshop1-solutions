# Pitch: Chore Roulette

## Script (90 seconds)

**[Speaker A, no slide, eye contact]**
**Hook (10s):** "It's 11pm. The bins are overflowing. Again. And everyone in the flat swears it isn't their turn."

**[Slide 2]**
**Problem (15s):** "Every student flat has this argument, every week. Chores land on the same person, and nobody remembers who did what."

**[Speaker B clicks: live demo]**
**Demo (40s):** "Meet Chore Roulette. We add our flatmates, Sam, Priya and Jonah, and our chores. Spin for bins... it's Jonah. Now watch what happens next week. Spin for bins again: Jonah's skipped, because he did them last week. That's the 'Fair play' rule, and it spreads every chore evenly. It all saves in the browser, with no login and no internet."

**[Slide 4]**
**How we built it (15s):** "We built it in under an hour with Claude Code. A CLAUDE.md pinned our stack and design, and our own skills planned the build, wrote this README and this pitch, and ran a go/no-go check before we walked up here."

**[Slide 5]**
**Close (10s):** "So tonight, nobody argues about the bins. The wheel decides."

## 5-slide outline
1. **The 11pm bin standoff:** photo of an overflowing bin
2. **Same person, every week:** one big stat-free line, "Who did the bins?"
3. **Demo (live):** the app on the projector
4. **Built with Claude Code:** CLAUDE.md, PLAN.md and the 4 skills, as a row of file icons
5. **The wheel decides:** the wheel, mid-spin
